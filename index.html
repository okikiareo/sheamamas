♥ About Us

Shea Mamas deploys decentralized renewable energy infrastructure to unlock sustainable productivity across women-led shea butter and local food processing value chains in Nigeria. Our solution replaces carbon-intensive energy sources with clean power — driving operational efficiency, income growth, food system resilience, enabling healthier livelihoods, improved productivity, and long-term shea environmental conservation.

Impact Programs

Clean Energy For Shea

The initiative deploys decentralized solar photovoltaic systems to power collective shea butter processing centers. Each site is designed for reliability, scalability, and productive use under rural operating conditions.

Impact Programs

Gender and Social Inclusion

Through gender-responsive programming, data-driven inclusion audits, and community-led partnerships, we advance measurable outcomes aligned with SDG 5 (Gender Equality), SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities), and SDG 16 (Inclusive Institutions). We prioritise safe participation spaces, equitable resource distribution, and representation in decision-making—creating systems that are resilient, fair, and capable of delivering long-term social and economic impact.

Impact Programs

Citizens’ Deliberation & Democratic Innovation Programme (CDDI)

Strengthening community voice in global decision-making through inclusive deliberation, civic learning, and participatory governance. The Citizens’ Deliberation & Democratic Innovation Programme is a civic participation and governance initiative designed to strengthen community voice, democratic literacy, and inclusive decision-making at local and global levels. The programme builds community capacity to participate meaningfully in structured deliberation processes, bridging grassroots perspectives into global policy conversations.
